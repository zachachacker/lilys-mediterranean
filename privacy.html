Privacy

The short version: we use your details to make and hand over your food, and to email you offers only if you join Lily's Club. We don't sell your information.

When you order online

We collect your name and phone number (so we can call you about your order), anything you write in the notes, what you order, and your address if you choose delivery. When you pay, Stripe asks for your email; we keep it to send your receipt and a "your order is ready" email.

Payments are handled by Stripe. Your card number goes to Stripe, never to us.

Lily's Club

If you tick "Join Lily's Club" or sign up on our club page, we email you offers and news about once a month. Every email has a one-tap unsubscribe link. We only email offers to people who joined.

Counting visits

Our own visit counts are anonymous daily totals: no cookies and no tracking of individual visitors. To see how customers find us, we note the website or link that brought you to us. To spot returning customers we store a scrambled code made from your phone number, which can't be turned back into the number.

Our pages also include Google's advertising tag, which helps us see whether our Google ads work. Google may set cookies for that; you can control them in your browser or at Google's ad settings.

Who else sees it

Only the services that run our ordering: Stripe (payments), Supabase (our order system) and Resend (our emails). We don't sell or rent your information to anyone.

Questions or deletion

Call us on (321) 312-4444 or visit us at 2 5th Ave STE C, Indialantic, FL 32903 and we'll help, including deleting your details where we're not required to keep order records.
